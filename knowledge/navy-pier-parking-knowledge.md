## Executive Summary

This Parking Agreement, effective February 5, 2016, provides Chicago Shakespeare Theater (CST) with discounted and complimentary parking access at Navy Pier to support its theatrical operations. Unlike a traditional lease, this is a parking access rights agreement that runs co-extensively with CST's underlying 1997 and 2016 theater leases. CST receives 15 free parking spaces year-round, plus the right to utilize up to 350 discounted spaces for Main Stage/Studio Theater performances and up to 75 spaces for Yard Theater performances (with seasonal limitations). All discounted parking is provided at 30% off Navy Pier's standard rates. The agreement includes coordination provisions for CST's patron parking, production/rehearsal parking, and special services like taxi coordination and disabled patron access.

The structure is operationally focused, requiring CST to provide quarterly parking forecasts and 24-hour advance confirmations for each performance. NPI retains flexibility to reassign unused spaces to public use, balancing CST's needs with Navy Pier's general parking operations. Notably, the agreement lacks many standard contract provisions including default definitions, remedies for breach (except CST's nuclear option to terminate the theater leases if parking cannot be provided for 12+ months), assignment rights, and maintenance obligations. The agreement provides significant value to CST by reducing patron parking costs and ensuring parking availability, which is crucial for theater attendance, particularly during Navy Pier's busy summer season.

## Key Risks & Opportunities

### Risks
- No explicit default or remedy provisions except CST's right to terminate theater leases after 12 months of parking unavailability - creates legal uncertainty
- Yard Theater parking limited to October-March with December blackouts and Winter WonderFest exclusion - restricts parking during peak season
- Production/rehearsal parking reduced from 52 to 17 spaces during summer (June 16-Aug 14) when operations are busiest
- No assignment or sublease provisions - unclear if CST can transfer rights or what happens if CST's operations change
- Parking spaces not reserved or guaranteed - subject to availability and NPI's operational needs
- Standard parking rates set unilaterally by NPI - even with 30% discount, costs could increase significantly
- CST has limited control over parking location - NPI designates specific spaces and can change locations
- No explicit force majeure provision - unclear what happens if parking facilities damaged or unavailable
- Automatic termination if either underlying theater lease expires - creates risk if one theater closes but other continues
- No specified insurance requirements or liability provisions - unclear who bears risk for patron injuries, vehicle damage
- 24-hour notice requirements may be difficult for last-minute performances or schedule changes

### Opportunities
- Significant cost savings: 15 free parking spaces plus 30% discount on up to 402 additional spaces
- Dedicated entrance and coordinated patron flow enhance customer experience
- Flexibility to utilize Performance Discount Parking across all three theaters (Main Stage, Studio, Yard)
- Opportunity to use Yard Theater discount parking outside permitted period when Navy Pier not at capacity
- Taxi and valet coordination services improve accessibility and patron convenience
- School bus and disabled patron drop-off coordination supports educational programming
- Unused Dedicated Spaces can be reassigned by NPI, removing pressure on CST to over-reserve
- Agreement runs co-extensive with theater leases, providing long-term parking certainty
- No base rent or CAM charges - only pay for excess discounts if tiered pricing exceeds 30% aggregate
- Right to terminate theater leases if parking unavailable for 12+ months provides leverage
- Quarterly reporting provides flexibility to adjust parking needs seasonally
